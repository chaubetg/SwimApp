FIRST — add it to your phone

Your times are saved on your phone, not on a server. Adding it to your home screen is what keeps them safe — a browser tab can clear them after a week of not visiting.

iPhone & iPad · Safari
1Tap the Share icon at the bottom, scroll down, then Add to Home Screen.
Android · Chrome
1Tap ⋮ at the top right, then Add to Home screen, then Install app in the box that appears. If Chrome offers to install it for you, take that instead — it does the same thing.
2Open it from the new icon and pick below — it remembers, and goes straight there next time.
