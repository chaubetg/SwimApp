Pacer — v1 (real pacing data)

Designed for use poolside — large hero numbers, 44pt+ touch targets, tabular figures, status communicated through icon + color + position (never color alone). Works for a coach on deck with their athlete or for an athlete planning their own race.

Design principles applied
  • • Glanceable hero numbers — split times are the largest type on screen
  • • Tabular figures — digits align vertically
  • • 44pt+ touch targets for pool-deck use
  • • Color + shape — every status has icon + color + position
  • • Plain language — coaching insights speak to the athlete
Real pacing data

Split ratios are the median + IQR of ~24 elite swims per event (Olympics & World Champs), derived from your World Aquatics dataset. Coverage: free 50–1500, back/breast/fly 50/100/200, IM 200/400 — Men & Women, LCM & SCM. Swimmers/races below are still sample data.
